Data and interpretation

What is counted?

NHTSA ADS reports identifying Waymo, no human driver/operator, and ADS engaged. Latest report versions are retained and shared incident IDs are deduplicated. All crash severities and reported locations are included.

What are the miles?

Quarterly differences between published cumulative rider-only mileage releases. Earlier releases sum published depot rows; later releases use the overall total. Historical revisions and changes in coverage can affect those differences.

What can this tell us?

These are reported crash involvements, regardless of fault. Exposure to different roads, cities, and conditions changes over time. The charts describe this dataset and do not establish that Waymo caused a crash or became safer.
