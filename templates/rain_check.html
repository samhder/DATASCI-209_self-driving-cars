What this cannot tell you

How far the cars droveNobody reports miles, so "safer in rain" and "mostly parked in rain" look exactly the same in the data.
Which streetsRegulators remove the exact location, so we can only answer for a whole city, not a neighborhood.
How hard it rainedA report says "rain" or not. Drizzle and a downpour count the same.
Data: NHTSA Standing General Order crash reports for automated driving systems, July 2021 to August 2026, de-duplicated to 2,945 crashes, and hourly rain for six metros from the Open-Meteo Historical Weather API. Expected rain crashes = a city's crashes × the share of hours it was raining there. Injury rates carry 95 percent intervals. Group 4, DATASCI 209, UC Berkeley MIDS; code in the weather_eda folder of the team repo.
